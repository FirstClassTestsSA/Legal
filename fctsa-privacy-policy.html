Privacy Policy

Firearm Competency Test SA
Published by First Class Tests SA
Effective date: 23 November 2026 (previous version: 22 November 2026)

What changed on 23 November 2026: if the price has not loaded yet (for example, after the app started offline), the app now asks for it again when you open the unlock screen or come back to the app. Nothing else changed.

In short: Firearm Competency Test SA works offline. It has no user accounts and no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no advertising or tracking tools and nothing that records what you do inside the app. It goes online automatically, when it starts with an internet connection, for two things only:
  • to check your unlock and its price with Google Play and RevenueCat (this check can run again when you come back to the app after a few minutes, and when you buy or restore the unlock; if the price has not loaded yet, the app also asks for it again when you open the unlock screen or come back to the app); Google Play may also check that the app was installed from Google Play; and
  • to check Expo for new question content.
Expo, our update provider, keeps a record of each update check and gives us counts of installations and failed updates; RevenueCat, our purchase provider, gives us statistics on purchases and on how many installations check in (see sections 3 and 5). These statistics are what Google Play's data safety section lists as "Analytics". None of this is used to identify you, build a profile of you or follow you across other apps or websites.

1. Who we are

This app, Firearm Competency Test SA (Google Play package za.co.fctsa.firearms), is published by First Class Tests SA ("we", "us"). For the purposes of the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), we are the responsible party (the one who decides why and how your information is used) for the limited processing described below. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

2. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect or ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise (GPS) location, or an advertising identifier. We do not build a behavioural profile of you and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. When the app goes online, and what is sent

The app makes two kinds of online check, described below. Both run automatically when the app starts with an internet connection, and the unlock check can run again when you return to the app. There is no setting to turn them off. While you are offline, nothing is sent and the app still works fully, but you will not receive new questions and cannot buy or restore the unlock. Buying is optional. Every connection the app makes reveals your device's IP address to the provider it reaches (Google, RevenueCat or Expo). All of these connections are encrypted (HTTPS). We do not operate our own servers.

(a) Your unlock — Google Play and RevenueCat.

  • RevenueCat manages your unlock (entitlement) for us. Each time the app starts, and again when you return to it after a few minutes, whether or not you ever buy, the app checks your unlock and the price with RevenueCat. If the price has not loaded yet (for example, after the app started offline), the app asks RevenueCat for it again when you open the unlock screen or come back to the app. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier that the app creates, together with technical details such as the app version, the RevenueCat software version, the operating-system version, the device model and your device's country (from the store), and it keeps a record for that identifier. The identifier contains no name or email address. If you buy or restore the unlock, the record also holds your purchase and entitlement records, including your Google Play order number. RevenueCat gives us statistics on purchases and on how many installations check in. We do not send your name, email or contacts to RevenueCat.
  • Google Play (Google) provides the price, processes your payment and links the purchase to your Google account. The app never sends us your card details or your Google identity. When the app opens, Google Play may also check that the app was installed from Google Play (Google Play's app-integrity protection); this check is part of Google Play and may contact Google.
  • What Google Play shows us. In Play Console (Google's website for app publishers), Google shows us a record of each purchase: the order number, date, product and price, your country and, for some sales, your state, city and postcode, and the device model. It also lets us find an order by the buyer's email address. We use this only for refunds, support, deletion requests and tax records.

(b) Content updates — Expo. Each time the app is opened fresh (not when you return to it from the background), it asks Expo (expo.dev), our update-delivery provider, whether new question content is available, and downloads it if so. These requests, and any download that follows, carry technical information such as:

  • the platform (Android);
  • technical labels that tell Expo which version of the app and which question content you have (the app's runtime version, its update channel, and the identifiers of the content built into the app and of the content currently running);
  • a user-agent string, which names the network software making the request;
  • a random installation identifier that the app creates the first time it runs. It is not your name, email address, phone number, Google account or a hardware identifier, and the app does not show it to you or link it to you;
  • any technical values that Expo's servers have asked the app to send back.

There are two kinds of failure report:

  • If the app ever crashes while starting a version of its content for the first time (usually a newly downloaded update), each later check also lists the identifiers of up to five such failed versions, until they are cleared from your phone.
  • Separately, if the update system cannot read its own saved files when the app starts and has to fall back to the content built into the app, the next check (once only) includes a short technical description of that error, at most about 1,000 characters.

Expo stores the installation identifier, your IP address and any failed-update identifiers with its records of these checks. It uses them on our behalf to decide which installations receive an update when we release it gradually, to count installations and failed updates for our update statistics, and to measure how much its update service is used. We use the failure statistics to spot and withdraw a faulty update. Expo states that it does not use this data for advertising, does not combine it with other apps' data, does not work out your location from it, and deletes the start-up error text after three days (see Expo's description of this data).

Clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Firearm Competency Test SA > Storage > Clear storage) makes the app create new identifiers for Expo and RevenueCat the next time it starts. It does not delete the records Expo or RevenueCat already hold; it only means future checks use new identifiers. Clearing storage also erases your study progress, bookmarks and any free questions or mock-exam credit earned by sharing; a purchased unlock comes back automatically the next time the app is online, or through the app's "Restore previous purchase" option. Uninstalling and reinstalling may not give new identifiers: if Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android restores the app's saved data, including the old identifiers, when the app is reinstalled.

4. Information kept on your device

Your study progress, quiz results and bookmarks are kept on your device and are never sent to us. If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android also includes them in your device backup in your Google account (held by Google, not by us) and restores them if you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. Your unlock is recorded by RevenueCat (section 3(a)), and the app keeps a copy on your device so that it works offline.

5. Why we process this information

We process the limited information above to provide the app; to process your purchase and unlock the paid content; to deliver content updates and keep statistics on the installations that check for them and on any failed updates; to receive statistics on purchases and installations from RevenueCat; to handle refunds, support requests and deletion requests and keep tax records (using Google Play's order records); and to meet legal obligations. Under POPIA, our lawful bases are the performance of our agreement with you (providing and unlocking the app), our legitimate interest in operating, maintaining, and improving the app, and compliance with legal obligations such as tax law.

6. Who processes it

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. RevenueCat and Expo process the information described above on our behalf, as our operators under POPIA (companies that handle information for us under contract), under the data-processing terms in their terms of service. Google processes your payment, provides Play Console and runs Google Play's checks as an independent responsible party under its own privacy policy.

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Expo: expo.dev/privacy (general policy) and its description of update-check data

7. International transfers

These providers operate servers outside South Africa, including in the United States, so your information may be processed in other countries. RevenueCat and Expo process it for us under the data-processing terms in their terms of service; Google processes payments under its own safeguards.

8. Retention

  • On your device: until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Any copy in your Android backup is kept under your Google account's backup settings.
  • Google: keeps its transaction records under its own policies. Any reports we download from Play Console for tax records are kept for as long as tax law requires.
  • RevenueCat: keeps its records for us until we delete them. Purchase records are kept for as long as needed for your purchase, refunds and tax records. We have not yet set an automatic deletion period for the records of installations that never bought anything.
  • Expo: keeps its update-check records (installation identifier, IP address and any failed-update identifiers) on our behalf and has not published how long it keeps them. The start-up error text is kept only in Expo's raw request logs, which Expo deletes after three days.
  • Emails you send us: see section 11.

9. Security

All of the app's connections to Google Play, RevenueCat and Expo are encrypted (HTTPS). We also reduce risk by minimising the information involved and by relying on the security measures of these providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

This app is intended for adults (18+) preparing for a statutory firearm competency assessment. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly process the personal information of children.

11. If you contact us

When you email us we receive your email address, your name as shown by your email provider, and whatever you include (for a deletion request, your order ID). We may use your email address to find your order in Play Console. We use this only to answer you and keep it only as long as we need to deal with your request. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).

12. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

  • Send requests about your information to us. We act on RevenueCat's records ourselves, and we pass requests about Expo's records to Expo.
  • Expo's update-check records hold a random installation identifier and your IP address (and any failed-update identifiers), but not your name or account, so we cannot look them up ourselves. We will ask Expo, which keeps them for us, whether it can find and delete them.
  • Google's records of your purchase and your Google account are Google's: use myaccount.google.com or contact Google.
  • Objecting. We cannot switch the automatic checks off for one phone. If you object, we will delete your RevenueCat record and ask Expo about its records, and you can keep using the app offline (without buying, restoring or new questions).
